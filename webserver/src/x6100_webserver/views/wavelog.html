Wavelog

Every FT8, FT4 and JS8 QSO logged on the radio is always kept on the SD card. With the upload on, new QSOs are also sent to your Wavelog logbook as soon as the radio can reach it, and wait in line while it cannot (Wi-Fi off, server down). With the upload off nothing is sent.
